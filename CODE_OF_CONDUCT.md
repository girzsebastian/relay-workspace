# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the code, not
about the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason. "This breaks on Windows because the
  path separator is hard-coded" is useful; "this is bad" is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. A closed issue is not an insult.
- Respect that people here work in different languages and time zones, and that
  most of them are doing this in spare hours.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about who someone is,
deliberate intimidation, publishing someone's private information, and
persistent unwelcome contact after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Open a private report through GitHub, or contact the maintainer directly through
their GitHub profile. Reports are read by the maintainer only. Say what
happened and link to it if it is public.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts, and
will say when they have done so.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit
messages — and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened to the parts this project actually needs.
